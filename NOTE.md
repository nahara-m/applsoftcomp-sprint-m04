### SemiAxisNotes

The scatter plot for the universities dataset show mostly expected groups with a few outliers. One axis is focused more on the academic vs professional focus of a given university based on its research and scholarly orientation on the positive side and its technical and vocational orientation on the negative side, which would separate the Ivy and elite private universities from the rest. For the second axis, I used the location setting of the university to capture city campuses and small college town or rural settings.

When checking by region, Midwest and South universities are low on both axes and placed in the bottom left. These are mostly community and tribal colleges, which mean they are outside metropolitan areas and not scholarly as many big 4-year universities. Still on the bottom left and more to the middle of the location setting, two Universities in the Northeast placed there are Service Academy ones. Smith College, which is a Womens college, is also around the middle, along with Vassar College, which a little higher on the elite axis, but still too low since it is a top liberal arts school. Specialized Arts universities are seen on the right side as they are located at city campuses and more focused, so somewhat vocational or professional. Curtis Institute of Music though is placed in the center instead of more to the right of the plot. 

All Ivy League schools are on the very top as they are scholarly and elite universities. Columbia is placed on the top right given it is an Ivy League in New York City. Harvard is the one with the highest score for the elite axis. Yale and Princeton are also on the top, along with other top universities like University of Chicago, Johns Hopkins, Berkeley, UCLA and NYU, which are elite and urban unis. While the state schools are mostly clustered in the middle, leaning more to the left since many of them are in college towns.

A few surprising observations are the for-profit schools, Walden and Strayer, which are placed on the top along with the more prestigious schools. In addition, an interesting note is that Georgia Tech is placed as more of a vocational/technical school. 

Overall, the scatter plot could capture distinct groups, but the for profit schools focus/style axis scores were too high. A third axis for domains could help better visualize the types of universities since some schools set with their type Tech are actually elite and academic too, and to capture the selective liberal arts and arts school.
